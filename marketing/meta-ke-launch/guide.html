The plan

The goal is sales. The test finds out which app and which ad sell; each sale is traced to its ad through the link it came from. One campaign, one audience, four ads, and Meta moves the money toward whichever ad people click.

Ad sets per product? Not on this budget. KES 2,000 split four ways is KES 500 each, too little for any of them to find its audience in a day. One ad set with four ads lets Meta pour the money into the ad people respond to, and the results still tell you which product that was. Split by product later, once one has proved itself.

1 · Set up once

About an hour, most of it waiting. Do this at least a day before the ads should run: new ads take up to 24 hours to be approved.

  1. A Facebook Page for Yarp Developers

    Ads run from a Page, not your profile. facebook.com/pages/create, category Software company. Use the YD logo, link yarpdevelopers.com/store, and make one post showing the apps so the Page doesn't look empty to people who tap it. Linking an Instagram account is optional; without one, Instagram ads show the Page's name.

  2. A business portfolio

    business.facebook.com → create a business portfolio named Yarp Developers, and add the Page to it. Running ads from a portfolio rather than a personal ad account means one flagged ad can't take your personal account down with it.

  3. An ad account in shillings

    In the portfolio: Settings → Accounts → Ad accounts → Add → Create a new ad account.
    • Country: Kenya · Currency: KES · Time zone: Africa/Nairobi
    • Currency and time zone can't be changed later. KES is what makes M-Pesa appear as a payment option; a USD account only takes cards.

  4. Pay with M-Pesa (prepaid)

    Ads Manager → Billing & payments → Add payment method. On a KES account with manual (prepaid) payments, Kenyan advertisers report M-Pesa among the options: you add funds with an M-Pesa prompt on your phone, and Meta spends from that balance and stops when it's empty. Add KES 2,500: Meta adds 16% VAT on ad spend in Kenya, so KES 2,000 of ads costs KES 2,320. If M-Pesa isn't offered, a Visa or Mastercard debit or prepaid card works instead (it's charged as the ads run, not in advance).

  5. Put the site changes live

    Before any ad runs, the store needs the version that records which ad each sale came from, and tells phone visitors the apps run on Windows. Store pages already default to KES.

2 · Build the campaign

In Ads Manager, with the new KES ad account selected. Anything not named here, leave at Meta's default.

  1. Campaign

    Create → objective Traffic → Continue. Name KE launch. Leave Special ad categories empty. The Employment category is for job offers, job boards and certification programmes; a CV-making app isn't one, and neither are the other three.

    Why Traffic when the goal is sales? Meta's Sales objective won't run unless the site sends Meta its purchases, through the Meta Pixel or from the server. The store deliberately sends Meta nothing. For one day it wouldn't help anyway: Sales needs about 50 purchases a week to learn from. Traffic sends people who click to the store. Your sales, and which ad each came from, are counted by the store itself (section 5).

  2. Budget and schedule

    Campaign budget (Advantage+ campaign budget) → Lifetime → KES 2,000. In the ad set, schedule a start and an end 24 hours apart, e.g. 06:00 to 06:00 the next day, Nairobi time. A lifetime budget over a fixed window is how you get exactly one day's spend.

  3. Ad set: where people go and what to count

    Name Kenya 18+. Conversion location: Website. Performance goal: Maximise number of link clicks. (Landing page views would need the Meta Pixel, which the site deliberately doesn't have.)

  4. Audience

    Locations: Kenya (people living in). Minimum age: 18. Keep Advantage+ audience on and, under audience suggestions, search for interests along these lines and add whichever Meta offers: Microsoft Windows, Microsoft Office, PDF, résumé or CV, job hunting, freelancing, creative writing, novels. They're a starting hint, not a limit; Meta widens from there.

  5. Placements

    Leave Advantage+ placements on. In 2026 Meta is removing the option to show ads only on desktop computers, so the ads will reach phones. That's handled: every ad says Windows PC, and a phone visitor sees how to buy with M-Pesa now and install later.

  6. Four ads

    For each ad in section 3: Identity → the Yarp Developers Page. Ad setup: Create ad → Single image. Upload both images; Meta uses the tall one for Stories and Reels (under Media, check each placement shows the right one). Paste the text, the Website URL and the URL parameters, and pick Learn more as the button. Turn off Advantage+ creative enhancements that add music or rewrite text; the image already has the price on it. Then duplicate the ad and swap in the next product.

  7. Publish, and check approval

    Publish the evening before the day. Each ad shows In review, then Active (or Scheduled). If one is rejected, the reason is in Account Quality; the usual fix is rewording a line that sounds like a claim about the person reading it.

3 · The four ads

The images are in marketing/meta-ke-launch/out/ in the website repo: upload them from there. Each link carries its own tag, so a sale shows which ad brought it.

4 · On the day

The first hours look noisy; don't touch anything before midday. In Ads Manager, add the columns Link clicks, CTR (link click-through rate) and CPC (cost per link click).

5 · Afterwards

  1. Count the sales

    Firebase console → Firestore → store_orders: each order from an ad has campaign.campaign = "ke-launch" and campaign.content naming the ad. The Paystack dashboard lists the same payments with their product.

  2. Compare clicks with sales

    Per product: KES spent (Ads Manager) against KES earned (orders). One day rarely pays for itself; what matters is which product got clicks and sales. That's the one to run for a week next.

  3. Switch the store back to euros by default

    In src/data/regions.js set DEFAULT_REGION = INTL and push. Visitors whose computer is on Nairobi time keep seeing shillings anyway.

  4. Refunds and questions

    Watch yarpdevelopers@gmail.com and the Page's inbox for a few days: ad buyers write to the Page. For a refund of an M-Pesa payment, email Paystack at support@paystack.com with the reference (yds_…), amount and date; it goes back to the buyer's M-Pesa. Card payments through Stripe are refunded from the Stripe dashboard.

Why this way

The goal is sales; the Meta setting is Traffic

Meta's objective only decides who it shows the ads to. Its Sales objective learns from purchases it can see, which needs the Meta Pixel on the site (breaking the store's no-trackers promise) or purchases sent to Meta from the server, and about 50 purchases a week before that learning works. A one-day test won't get near that. Traffic finds people who click from the first hour, and the tagged links show which clicks became sales. Once one product has proved itself and runs for a week or more, sending purchases to Meta from the server and switching to Sales is worth revisiting.

Most clicks come from phones

Desktop-only targeting is being removed from Meta ads in 2026, and most Kenyans on Facebook are on their phones. So the ads say Windows PC up front, and the store tells phone visitors they can pay with M-Pesa now (the key and download link come by email) or send the page to themselves on WhatsApp to try on their computer.

Prices on the image

KES 350 for a PDF signer is the hook in a market used to paying for subscriptions in dollars. Putting the price and "Pay with M-Pesa" on the image means the people who click already know it's affordable and local.
